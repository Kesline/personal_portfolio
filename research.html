MACHINE LEARNING · MULTI-OBJECTIVE OPTIMIZATION

Optimizing 3D printing process parameters

Investigated data-driven optimization of PLA 3D printing using regression models and NSGA-II. The work explored relationships between process settings and outcomes, balancing competing objectives while searching for promising parameter configurations.

Random ForestNSGA-IIPythonAdditive Manufacturing

Research context: Undergraduate Research Fellowship, UMass Boston. Supervisor: Prof. Kenneth K. Fletcher.

REPRODUCIBILITY · RECOMMENDATION SYSTEMS

Reproducing a collaborative parameter recommender

Reproduction study of a collaborative process-parameter recommendation approach for 3D printing. Implemented and evaluated an alternating least squares baseline and explored sequential recommendation rounds.

ALSRecommender SystemsEvaluation

Selected experiment: training RMSE 0.0227; held-out RMSE 0.1013. These are reproduction-run metrics, not publication claims.
