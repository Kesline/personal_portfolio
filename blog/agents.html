Building AI agents that are useful in practice

Draft for review · 2026

Starter article based on project themes. Review and personalize before publishing.

Projects like Atlas Explorer and CultureQuest have given me a practical way to think about what it means for an AI assistant to be useful.

The question behind the work

A polished interface is important, but usability also depends on asking the right questions, handling uncertainty, and keeping users in control. Small interaction decisions can make an assistant feel more reliable and easier to work with.

What comes next

I am continuing to explore how these systems can become more dependable, context-aware, and genuinely helpful.
