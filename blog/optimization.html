What 3D printing taught me about optimization

Draft for review · 2026

Starter article based on project themes. Review and personalize before publishing.

As part of my work on 3D printing process parameters, I began exploring how machine learning can help navigate a surprisingly complicated design space.

The question behind the work

One of the challenges is that improving one goal may make another worse. A model can predict outcomes, but it does not by itself decide which trade-off matters most. That is where multi-objective optimization becomes useful.

What comes next

The experience made me more interested in questions that sit between prediction, decision-making, and real-world constraints.
