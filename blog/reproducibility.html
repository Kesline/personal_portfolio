Why reproducing research matters

Draft for review · 2026

Starter article based on project themes. Review and personalize before publishing.

Reproducing existing work is a different challenge from building a project from scratch. It asks whether the underlying assumptions, data processing, and evaluation can be reconstructed with care.

The question behind the work

In a collaborative recommender reproduction study, I used an alternating least squares baseline and compared performance across known and held-out observations. Small differences in splits or metrics can tell different stories about how well a method generalizes.

What comes next

The most valuable lesson is to document not only what worked, but what remains uncertain and what another researcher would need in order to repeat it.
